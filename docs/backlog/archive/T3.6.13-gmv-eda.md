## EDA findings (2026-08-15)

Real data on `train.parquet` (250K users, aggregated by user_id):

- Zero users (GMV_total = 0): **12.33%** (NOT 45.9% as initially assumed)
- 50% percentile: 388.19
- 90% percentile: 2764.38
- 95% percentile: 4305.05
- 99% percentile: 9680.50
- 99.9% percentile: 24373.98
- Outliers (> 99.9%): 250 users

**Note**: The 45.9% zero figure cited in the original ticket comes from
`sample_submit.csv` (initial predictions for unknown users), not from
aggregated user-level GMV in train.parquet. The actual train set has
12.33% zero-GMV users — still high enough to justify zero-inflated
modeling (T3.6.7 ZILN baseline).
