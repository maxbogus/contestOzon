## Hypothesis

The target (Feb 14 - Mar 15 2026) is a gift season: people buy in ADVANCE (up to
~2 weeks before Feb 23 / Mar 8), at the LAST MINUTE, or LATE. A user's timing
pattern is a stable trait that should transfer 2025 -> 2026. The SHAPE (shares)
of gift-season buying is new signal vs the existing feb_mar_gmv_2025 total.

## Features (behind ECUP26_BEHAVIOR_FEATURES=1, OFF in production)

Non-overlapping partition of the 2025 gift season (Feb 14 - Mar 15):
- adv_feb23 [Feb14-20], lm_feb23 [Feb21-23], adv_mar8 [Feb24-Mar5], lm_mar8 [Mar6-8], late [Mar9-15]
- per window: log1p(gmv) + flags (5+5 gmv + 3 aggregate flags)
- shares: planner_share / lastmin_share / late_share (sum to 1)
- mass-buying baseline: gmv_pre_sep1_2025, gmv_pre_ny_2025, gift_vs_mass_2025

## Expectations

Directionally-consistent positive gain on BOTH folds (timing trait is stable),
NO sign-flip. Effect estimated ~0.0001-0.0003 (below the CatBoost +0.00087 win).
