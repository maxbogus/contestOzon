## Hypothesis

46%% of users have zero 30d GMV; the blend predicts them pred_log ~1.42 (bias
-1.42) and they carry ~50%% of total error^2. Positives are near-calibrated
(+0.84). The additive log-space bias (T3.8.6) was neutral because zero and
positive errors cancel globally. A MULTIPLICATIVE log-space shrink c<1
compresses all predictions toward 0 - fixing the zero over-prediction while
keeping the positives close (0.95*3.31 ~ 3.14 vs true 3.3).

DECISION: the target (gift season) is most like fold G (zero-heavy, +0.020), but the
fold-P flip is a risk. Submit BOTH the plain blend AND shrink0.95; use the public LB
(20% users) as the regime probe, then pick the final for the private LB.
